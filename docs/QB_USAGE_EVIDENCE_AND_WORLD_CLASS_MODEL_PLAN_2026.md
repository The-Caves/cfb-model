# CFB 2026: QB identity recovery and long-run model development

## Problem and operational meaning

The product needs to *model the quarterback who is likely to play*, not falsely claim an official confirmation. The actual live V03 scoring model still has only 14 aggregate team features. A separate QB-conditional forecasting pipeline already reads actual named-player pass EPA, treats QB1/QB2/QB3 as independent scenarios and has no automatic negative rank penalty. As of the last full capture, 206 of 230 upcoming team-game slots had a projected player, while zero met the strict game-specific reviewed official-starter standard. Model projection and official confirmation are separate fields.

## October 29 proposed-starter refinement (this branch)

1. **Repeated observed QB usage:** derive `recent_two_game_leading_passer_proxy` if the same named QB dominated passing-play use in both of the two most recent distinct football dates. Keep these dated, before-cutoff and source-observed; no confirmation is inferred.
2. **Incomplete player attribution:** refuse to identify a dominant player when fewer than 70% of known pass plays have a named passer. Do not trust a 100% share of a tiny *named* subset as a complete QB game.
3. **Outdated chart conflicts:** when chart QB1 and last-game passer differ, accept a *low-confidence* expected replacement only with two consecutive dominant passer games and an undated chart, or a *medium-confidence* projection if the dated depth chart demonstrably predates the newer actual game. A clearly newer chart conflict remains unresolved, not silently overwritten. Explicit pre-decision OUT/CONFLICT evidence blocks choosing an inactive recent QB.
4. **Verified QB1 out, no recent passer:** use the first other charted QB not listed OUT as a **low-confidence candidate**, never a confirmed starter. Preserve the alternate scenario list and any missing player sample.
5. **No phantom player downgrade:** the existing `qb_conditional_forecast.py` uses the prospective candidate's actual prior passing EPA, sample-size shrinkage, and a fitted team-scoring coefficient. It does not penalize a player simply for a QB2/QB3 depth rank. If there is no trustworthy sample, emit PASS/no adjustment and retain uncertainty.

This improves inference, not source attestations or historical betting validation. Unit tests cover consecutive usage, chart conflict, injury overrides, low named-pass coverage, and intact strict confirmation boundaries. Run the entire CI suite before merging and an actual `[run-intake]` workflow to measure the change against the previous 206/230 expected-starter checkpoint.

## Starter-evidence tiers

- **Confirmed for this game:** independently reviewed, explicitly dated official team / on-record coach statement naming who starts the **specific** upcoming fixture. Source, capture, publication and review timestamps must all precede the forecast. Contradictions quarantine the claim.
- **Likely/projection:** timestamped ESPN game-specific historical participation, team-athletics reports describing recent health or role, Ourlads third-party depth chart, pregame availability records and multiple-game QB usage evidence. Do not call this confirmed.
- **Unresolved:** missing/contradictory/time-invalid data; preserve plausible player-conditioned scenarios and the unmodified V03 fallback.
- **Postgame evidence:** independently verify the actual first QB/snap share and offensive output, but never leak a postgame starting identity back to a frozen pregame forecast.

## October 29: empirical QB participation-exposure shadow, milestone 2A

The first automated QB mixture now runs inside the existing **frozen** `qb_conditional_forecast.py` research pipeline, without changing V03 live site scores. Its inputs are captured and sourced predecision:

- Prefer reviewed exact-game starter only when that independently accepted evidence exists. No third-party rank or recent-game passer can create an official confirmation.
- Otherwise, compute 2:1 recency-weighted *historical pass-play exposure shares* over the latest two distinct fully observed prior games, with source coverage at least 70% of all team pass plays. This is a descriptive share, **not** `P(QB starts)`. Unknown/untagged/other players and newly unavailable QBs retain an explicit UNKNOWN exposure mass.
- Condition each candidate's points on existing sample-shrunk historical player EPA and the frozen trained V03 passing coefficient, not depth-chart ordinal rank. Missing player samples and UNKNOWN exposure contribute **zero adjustment to the original V03 baseline**, not a fabricated impact.
- Freeze `usage_weighted_home_margin` and the original single-expected-starter `adjusted_home_margin` **side by side** in the same immutable research archive with the underlying exposure weights and qualifying sample evidence. The independent prospective candidate grader evaluates `qb_empirical_exposure` against the *same selected frozen games* as the existing QB shadow and V03.
- A shadow is not better because it is new. It must beat the correctly time-matched V03 and other QB challengers prospectively; no automatic promotion. Historical passer usage can be a weak proxy for who will start after an injury/transfer.

### What milestone 2A does not claim

The new exposure share is **not trained or calibrated to true starting-quarterback outcomes**. Historical first-snap QB labels are not reliably authenticated or available for all games yet. The next phase is to collect legitimate postgame first-snap/actual-starter labels and full pregame official coaching and school evidence, freeze time-stamped model inputs, and walk-forward train + calibrate game-specific `P(QB starts)`. Only then should the UI or model display calibrated starting probabilities. For uncertainty-sensitive ATS/OU recommendations, continue research-only/PASS until those gates succeed.

## October 29: actual-starter label capture and append-only postgame review (milestone 2B)

The new `qb_postgame_starter_labels.py` uses the ESPN completed-game summary strictly for observed **QB starter annotations**, not passing leader or passing-play volume. It checks the source's two team names, home/away identities, final scores, completed status, and optional ESPN game ID before accepting the player's literal boolean `starter` or `isStarter` flag **with a QB-position match**. If that verified field is not present, the label remains `UNKNOWN_NOT_VERIFIED_STARTER`. Two distinct positively flagged QBs are a conflict requiring manual review.

The `qb_postgame_starter_archive.py` captures dated postgame observations in `context-state/research_2026/qb_postgame_starters/` with source URLs, response digests, and an append-only per-game/team ledger. Re-observing the same outcome does not multiply a label. UNKNOWN may mature into an explicit sourced positive label. Any subsequent disagreement or disappearance of an accepted label is quarantined, with no automatic training use. Artifacts preserve the capture review reports. Data Intake backfills the season on an explicitly triggered push and then scans a bounded recent window, while the daily final-settlement workflow checks completed games in its existing 28-day window.

**Training is not unblocked by code alone.** We need to measure the live ESPN field coverage; if the source lacks explicit lineup flags, the honest count is zero. Postgame school gamebooks or officially published lineups can be evaluated next for explicit QB starts under independently reviewed source rules. Before training starter probabilities, create an audited feature-to-label table where every feature was archived before kickoff and each selected label is independently established after completion. Neither historical passer EPA nor a depth-chart ranking is itself the true starter label.

## Explicit official university boxscore starter-label recovery

Live ESPN postgame QB evidence backfill across **570 actual completed games (1,140 game-team slots)** found **zero provider-stated starting quarterbacks**. This was correctly flagged UNKNOWN, not mislabeled from the player with the most passing snaps.

A separate game-specific fallback now parses **official university 2026 football boxscore STARTERS tables** (the offensive roster's row with position `QB`). The game identity, independent final-score pair, game date and exact publisher hostname are reviewed in `config/official_school_qb_boxscores_2026.json`. The first reviewed historical sources are the University of Texas's Oct 24 Ole Miss boxscore, Alabama's Oct 24 Texas A&M boxscore, and Texas Tech's Oct 24 Cincinnati boxscore. Texas Tech was the away team, and its published scoreboard lists home/away results in the opposite order; that difference is explicitly documented per URL, never assumed by the parser.

`scripts/qb_official_school_boxscores.py` is separate from the ESPN JSON provider. Accepted results enter the same append-only `qb_postgame_starters` ledger, where official positive labels are **not invalidated by a subsequent ESPN record that lacks a starter field**. Conflicting positive names or rescinded claims from an identical official source remain quarantined. A new inexpensive, on-registry-change GitHub Action rechecks only reviewed fixture dates, without rerunning the entire prediction model. Live university source readability and audited capture counts must be checked from workflow output before claiming broader coverage.

This is a **seed and verifiable source pathway**, not nationwide starter coverage. Do not train a starter probability classifier on a handful of manually reviewed university source pages. Expand official gamebook coverage to 138 FBS schools, build immutable forecast-time feature/actual-starter joins, audit selection bias from schools with accessible gamebooks, and then use genuinely held-out chronological games to calibrate probabilities.

## Official school starter parser integrity finding (October 30 capture)

The first live source check accepted **six positive QB roster rows across three official games**. An independent archive audit caught **three contaminated player strings** caused by embedded team-logo image `alt` text leaking into the university roster's name field. Examples were `Arch Ole Miss Manning` and `Keelon Texas A&M Russell`, neither of which is a real player name. The original archived bytes are preserved and *must not be treated as clean training labels*.

The parser now tags image `alt` tokens separately from literal player-name cells and refuses to promote any unique QB starter unless it matches the exact manually reviewed QB name in the source registry. A narrowly scoped audited correction manifest `config/official_school_qb_name_corrections_2026.json` lists **only the three captured defects**, original provider payload SHA-256, published official school URL, old contaminated name, and reviewed corrected name. The archive writer may append a reviewed correction only when all those fields match the previously stored source and the newly fetched corrected official row; it keeps the previous observation and adds an explicit `reviewed_adjudication` trail. Every other unexpected starter-name change still quarantines the slot. Independent prospective model training must use only the currently effective, non-quarantined verified labels and exclude older contaminated observations as ground truth.

## October 29: audit pregame QB forecasts against independently verified starters (milestone 2C)

`scripts/qb_starter_supervised.py` now prepares a **prospective starter-classification research dataset** by joining two separately authenticated GitHub archives:

- `context-state/research_2026/candidate_shadow_qb/index.json`: source-digested, frozen QB expected-starter and individual-player scenario snapshots observed before kickoff. Pick the **latest archived forecast in the fixed 24–72-hour pregame window**, never the best hindsight outcome. Every scenario includes predecision rank, projected starter source and confidence, historic play sample / EPA, and the explicitly uncalibrated recent usage-share proxy.
- `context-state/research_2026/qb_postgame_starters/index.json`: append-only, game/team/source-verified **postgame actual starter labels**. Verify the archive record SHA and individual observed row, do not use passing leader, and never admit evidence captured after the evaluation cutoff.
- For official university gamebooks, the accepted player's normalized name and exact source URL **must match the reviewed school gamebook registry**, preventing known team-logo alt-text contamination from becoming labels. Conflicted or unknown results, future labels, and verified QB names not present in the frozen candidate set cannot be silently converted into supervised examples.
- A grouped training example is an actual game/team with one postgame starter and all candidates **as named before kickoff**, not one independently countable sample per player's row. Controlling label dates, missing-source and no-coverage cohorts is mandatory before calibration.

A daily final-settlement job checks out the immutable `context-state` Git tree *after* any source-archive writes and emits an auditable status/coverage JSON and example rows without modifying frozen forecasts. Local checked-out bytes are SHA-verified against the archive indexes, so the supervised audit doesn't depend on dozens of per-file GitHub REST requests (which can receive rate-limit HTTP 403s). Archive writes remain separate and require their own successful GitHub permissions/rate-limit checks. This module **does not train or publish probabilities**. The initial training gate requires at least 80 truly labeled game-team examples spanning four distinct football dates and at least 75% of labeled starters present in the pregame candidate slate. These thresholds are preliminary minimum safeguards, not validation or acceptance criteria for a betting model.

**Current important blocker:** at the October 29 audit, the ledger contained 6 accepted observations out of 1,140 team-game slots, three of which had an already-detected team-logo alt-text name corruption pending its append-only reviewed correction. All six concerned October 24 games, but the available prospective QB shadow records begin October 29. Therefore the **initial matched supervised sample is zero**, not six. Future independently labeled outcomes and reviewed school sources are required before a calibrated starter model can honestly be trained.

## October 30: prioritize actual-starter source review using frozen pregame forecasts

The daily settlement's `qb_starter_supervised.py` now also writes a `qb-starter-source-review-<run>.csv` artifact. Only completed game/team slots that have **both** an independently archived postgame score-evidence slot and an immutable 24–72-hour pregame QB scenario enter this prioritized worklist. Unknown/conflicted/uncorrected official source names or verified actual starters missing from the pregame roster remain explicit reason-coded targets, never new training labels. Already trainable slots and games without reliable postgame completion evidence do not appear. Candidate names on the list are only names present in the old frozen prediction, not an inferred true starter. Exact gamebook URLs stay blank until individually reviewed. This makes the source-review workload relevant to prospective starter classification and avoids retrospective fabrication.

## Next development milestones, in testable order

1. **National QB source coverage:** expand verified official football preview/article and direct game-note PDF sources beyond the few currently readable feeds. Capture dated full text and exact fixture, not just an RSS homepage or metadata. Add a separate official availability update pipeline with conflict/review signals. Measure confirmation coverage by game week and report source failures.
2. **QB state estimator:** train an as-of, game-specific, probabilistic starting-player forecast using chart ranking, observed game usage, dated practice/coach/availability, transfer and known replacement/return events; retain calibrated `P(QB starts)`, reliability, contradictions and a genuine UNKNOWN mass. Learn weights from prospectively saved predictions and independently verified postgame labels; do not invent calibrated values while training data are absent. High-impact uncertainty should return scenario ranges or PASS.
3. **QB performance conditional on opposition:** for each credible QB compute opponent- and pressure-adjusted pass EPA, accuracy/completion over expectation where measurable, explosive rates, sack avoidance, scramble efficiency, red-zone performance, and recent form; estimate a hierarchical/shrunken player plus offensive-support contribution. Players with no prior play-by-play are assessed using explicit transfer or historical prior with large uncertainty, not automatically “0-point effect.”
4. **Run/pass matchup engine:** candidate features should quantify offense style against opponent defense and environment, including fronts/OL mismatches, pressured dropbacks, pressure response, opponent-adjusted rushing success, red zone, explosive-play suppression and verified turnovers. Use time-versioned features and missingness indicators. Test new features *one family at a time* against the recency-corrected and active reference model before combining.
5. **Generative scoring distribution:** model possessions, plays/drive, field position, drive points and non-offensive scoring; simulate joint team-score distributions with pace and explosive-play uncertainty. Derive SU, margin, ATS and totals distributions consistently from that joint model. Test against a simple calibrated ridge and market consensus.
6. **Calibration and markets:** archive real predecision sportsbook prices with observed timestamps and provider identity, use market spread/total as an explicit baseline, measure MAE, log loss/Brier, reliability, interval coverage, ATS/O-U performance and vig-adjusted ROI only when price-side evidence exists. Keep opening/closing attribution honest; never fabricate.
7. **Walk-forward challenger gate:** freeze forecasts 24–72h or chosen fixed lead before kickoff, sample one decision per game/version, use game-disjoint weekly evaluation with predecision snapshots, independent final scores, uncertainty intervals and minimum coverage. Track performance by conference, QB status, week, home/away, market-disagreement band, weather and source-missing cohort. Correct the known original V03 home-team bias; avoid post hoc winner selection.
8. **Operations:** monitor intake schedules, fixture and publisher changes, data latency/failures, forecast/grade archives and alerting. No scheduled run should silently promote candidate features or erase prior picks.

## Explicit completion criteria

- Verification pipeline passes unit/regression tests and live intake.
- A starter expectation is emitted for every source-supported fixture; unsupported cases remain unresolved and each model forecast retains plausible conditional alternatives. Official confirmations are **only** reported when genuinely documented.
- Player/QB effects and new game-level features beat the recency-controlled baseline on *independent prospective* matched games, with no worsening of key calibration / home-bias diagnostics. Historical retrospectively mutable PBP alone never qualifies.
- Model promotion remains a separate reviewed change, never a side effect of changing labels, dashboard cards or an impressive in-sample comparison.
